Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What happens if I exceed my monthly video limit?

If you exceed your monthly limit, you won't be able to process new videos until the next billing cycle. However, you can:

  • Upgrade to a higher tier immediately
  • Purchase additional credits
  • Wait until your limit resets

Free tier users have the option to upgrade to continue processing.

How does silent video detection work?

Our system automatically analyzes audio tracks using FFmpeg to detect if a video contains speech. Videos without speech:

  • Skip expensive transcription services
  • Use cheaper AI models for analysis
  • Save 69% on processing costs

This happens automatically - you don't need to do anything!

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. When you cancel:

  • You'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period
  • Your account will downgrade to Free tier automatically
  • You'll keep all your processed videos (subject to retention periods)
  • You can resubscribe anytime
What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards through Stripe:

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express
  • Discover, JCB, Diners Club
  • Apple Pay, Google Pay

Enterprise customers can also pay via invoice (net 30 terms).

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Yes! All paid plans include a 14-day free trial with full access to all features. During the trial:

  • No credit card required to start
  • Full access to all plan features
  • Process up to 10 videos for testing
  • Cancel anytime during trial with no charge

After the trial, you'll be asked to enter payment details to continue.

How do upgrades/downgrades work?

Upgrading or downgrading is easy and immediate:

  • Upgrades: Take effect immediately. You'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle.
  • Downgrades: Take effect at the end of your current billing period. You'll keep access to higher tier features until then.
  • Yearly to Monthly: You can switch anytime, but yearly discounts won't apply to monthly billing.
